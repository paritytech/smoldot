Module peer_steering

Peer steering of a light client, the port of the peer_steering module of the full node’s statement protocol.

The steering keeps the statement links of a chain aligned with the peers needed to cover the subscribed topics. It keeps three sets of peers: the peers with an open statement substream, the needed peers, and the reserved peers, the ones that the caller holds desired under the statement gossip kind. Each refresh moves the reserved set toward the needed one. PeerSteering::peers_to_reserve lists the needed peers to mark as desired, which dials them and keeps their statement substream open. PeerSteering::peers_to_release lists the reserved peers no longer needed. A refresh releases at most a fifth of the connected peers, so that the set converges over several refreshes rather than churning at once. The cover of the topics comes from the peers topology through PeerSteering::update_peers_needing_connections.

Besides the cover, RANDOM_SLOTS links go to peers chosen at random among the known statement peers, so that an attacker who surrounds a topic with peers of its own never holds every link.

A peer that refuses a link, or that the caller bans, is left alone until its ban ends, so that it is not opened again at once. Statements keep flowing over the statement substreams that follow the block announces substreams of the gossip peers. The steering reserves such a peer like any other, and releasing it leaves its substream to the block announces substream.

The full node scores each link by the statements the peer delivers and drops the lowest scores first. A light client validates statements outside of the network service, so the links are dropped longest-held first.
