Module peers_topology 

Source
Expand description

Event-fed local view of the statement peers of a chain, the light client’s port of the peers_topology module of the full node’s statement protocol.

Every peer is mapped into the 32-byte topic key space by the blake2b-256 hash of its PeerId, and the distance to a topic is the XOR of the two keys. The full nodes keep a topic on the replication_factor peers closest to it, so a light client that connects to one of the closest known peers for a topic is likely to reach a holder of that topic.

The topology is built from peers learned through discovery, Identify and statement substreams, and remembers them with their addresses after they disconnect, so that a peer close to a topic can be connected again later, even once the address book shared with the block sync has dropped it. It computes XOR distances locally over that learned peer set and never issues a topic-specific lookup, so that nobody learns which topics the local node subscribes to.

The full node counts a peer as a statement peer only once Identify confirms it. A light client connects to a handful of peers and gets no Identify answer from the other ones, so a discovered peer counts as a statement peer until Identify or a refused statement substream says otherwise.

Only a full node on the DHT path speaks statement/3. A peer on an older version wants every statement and is no candidate for a topic, unless no known peer speaks statement/3.

A light client is never a replica of a topic, so the affinity oracle and the routing of statements between replicas of the full node have no counterpart here.
